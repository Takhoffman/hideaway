# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on [Tak Hoffman's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Takhoffman). Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
